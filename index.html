HERITAGE & SAVOIR-FAIRE

A Century of Uncompromising Artistry

Founded in 1924 along Paris's iconic Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, Maison d'Élite has preserved the rare artisanal techniques of French Haute Couture across generations.

Every single garment that bears the Maison d'Élite mark is constructed by hand by artisan tailors with decades of mastery. From hand-dyed Italian silks to conflict-free precious gemstones, our materials are sourced with rigorous ethical standards.
